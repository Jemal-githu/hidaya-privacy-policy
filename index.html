Privacy Policy for Hidaya

Last updated: July 2026

Hidaya ("the app", "we", "our") is an Islamic lifestyle app developed to help Muslims with prayer times, Quran study, dua, halal/boycott scanning, health tracking, and Islamic learning for kids. This policy explains what information the app accesses, why, and how it is handled.

No account required

Hidaya does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email, or any personal identifier to use the app. Any name or profile you enter (e.g. a Kids Learning profile) is stored only on your device.

Our commitment: using Hidaya's core features will never require an account. If we introduce optional accounts in the future (for example, to back up your progress or sync between devices), they will remain strictly optional, and this policy will be updated before any such feature launches.

Data stored on your device only

The following information is stored locally on your phone and is never uploaded to any server we control:

  • Prayer time settings, calculation method, and location coordinates used to calculate prayer times
  • Quran reading progress, bookmarks, memorization (Hifz) progress, and daily reading goals
  • Health Monitor data: step counts, heart rate readings, water intake, weight/BMI entries
  • Kids Learning profiles: child names/avatars you create, story progress, quiz scores, and stars
  • Scanned/learned product barcodes (Sumud Scan / Halal Scan) that you personally identify
  • App theme, language, and other preferences

If you uninstall the app, this locally stored data is deleted along with it.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Location: to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your area. Location is used locally on-device and is not transmitted to us.
  • Camera: to scan product barcodes/labels (Sumud Scan, Halal Scan), to measure heart rate via the camera lens (Health Monitor), and to scan printed mosque prayer timetables (OCR). Photos are processed on-device or sent only to the specific third-party service needed to complete that feature (see below) and are not stored by us.
  • Microphone: for the Tajweed recitation feedback feature and the Live Khutba Translator, which need to hear your voice or the sermon being translated. Audio is processed for the immediate purpose only and is not stored by us.
  • Notifications: to deliver athan (prayer call) alerts, prayer time reminders, and weekly Kids Learning progress summaries.
  • Health Connect / Activity Recognition: to read step count and other fitness data from Health Connect (if you choose to connect a smartwatch or fitness app) for the Health Monitor feature.
  • Storage (photos/files): to let you import a scanned mosque timetable file or PDF Quran, and to save/share content you create.

Third-party services used by specific features

Some features send data to third-party services solely to perform that feature's function:

  • Barcode/product lookup: product barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts, Open Beauty/Products Facts, and UPCitemdb to identify products.
  • Boycott data: brand/company boycott information is downloaded from the public Tech For Palestine dataset.
  • AI features (Premium): Tajweed recitation feedback and Live Khutba Translation use speech recognition and translation services (including on-device and Google services) to process audio into text/translations. Audio is not retained after processing.
  • Quran audio/text: Quran recitations, translations, and tafsir content are fetched from public Quran content APIs (e.g. alquran.cloud, Tech-for-Palestine-hosted tafsir mirrors) as needed.

These third parties process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers, and Hidaya contains no advertising and no third-party ad trackers.

Children's privacy (Kids Learning)

The Kids Learning module lets a parent create local profiles for their children on the parent's own device. No data about a child is collected by us or transmitted off the device — profiles, stories completed, and stars earned are stored locally only, managed entirely by the parent.

Data deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by removing a profile/entry within the app, or by uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data immediately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
